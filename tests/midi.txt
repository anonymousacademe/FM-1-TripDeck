# MIDI in over the TRS jack ("uart" = raw bytes on the jack, parsed as firmware/src/midi_uart.c does) and MIDI out over USB.
#   .\fm1.ps1 host tripdeck tests/midi.txt > build\host\midi-trace.txt ; python tests\check_midi.py build\host\midi-trace.txt
# A  running status: one status byte, three notes (C4 E4 G4 at velocity 100)
100 uart 90 3C 64 40 64 43 64
# B  note offs with a clock byte in the middle of messages (real time may come anywhere)
600 uart 80 3C 00 F8 40 00 43 F8 00
# C  a SysEx on the jack is ignored; the note after it plays (C5, velocity 80); note on with velocity 0 is a note off
900 uart F0 7E 7F 06 01 F7 90 48 50
1100 uart 90 48 00
# D  program change picks patch 4 (index 3)
1300 uart C0 03
# E  pitch wheel: up to the top, then back to the middle
1500 uart E0 7F 7F
1600 uart E0 00 40
# F  all notes off (CC 123) also goes out over USB
1800 uart 90 3C 64 90 3E 64
2100 uart B0 7B 00
# G  the arpeggiator plays an external chord: its notes go out over USB-MIDI
2400 btn 10
2500 uart 90 3C 64 90 40 64 90 43 64
4300 uart 80 3C 00 80 40 00 80 43 00
4400 btn 10
4500 btn 10
# H  a panel key with the arp off goes out directly (F3 = note 53, velocity 100)
5000 keys 0x1
5200 keys 0
# I  the sequencer's notes go out (the default line starts with an accent on A2 = 45)
5600 btn 12
7000 btn 12
8000 end
